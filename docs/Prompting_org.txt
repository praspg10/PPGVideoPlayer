6. "Increase branding size a little more (to 18%). Move AST Counter to 18-28% slot."

20. "In settings, if a folder has text '-skipscan', ignore that folder's videos and its subfolders during scan/rescan."
21. "In the settings popup, next to the Recent row, add a row to enable/disable the skipscan implementation (Disabled by default)."
